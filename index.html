WHO WE ARE

Making the world a little kinder.

Conceptualised in 2014, PAWS – Protection of Animals & Welfare Society – works to protect vulnerable, abandoned and homeless animals in Jaipur. Our mission is to bring practical compassion into everyday life through feeding, adoption, seasonal relief, education and community participation.

We believe every animal deserves dignity and care, whether they have a home or not.

MEET OUR FOUNDER

Viren Sharma

27 years of passion, purpose and pioneering canine culture

Founder of PAWS and a leading force behind International Dog Bazar, Viren Sharma has helped connect Rajasthan's growing canine community with a commitment to responsible pet parenting and care for homeless animals.

His involvement as founder secretary of the Kennel Club of Rajasthan has helped promote dog shows, education and public participation. Through PAWS, his vision extends compassion beyond pedigree dogs to every voiceless animal in need.

“I don't feed these dogs, I feed my own soul.”
Read more about Viren's journey & recognitions

Building on a family enterprise dating to 1957, Viren has championed professional pet care, education, organised dog shows, animal adoption and street-animal welfare. His supplied biography lists honours including Rajasthan Gaurav Award, Jaipur Ratna Award, Samaj Ratna Award, National Pet Icon Award, World Records of India recognition and other industry and social awards.
